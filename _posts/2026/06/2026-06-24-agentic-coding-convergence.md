This historical note is merged into [HarnessRouter Standardizes Tasks, Not Execution Guarantees](/posts/harnessrouter-execution-contract/), whose pinned source audit already distinguishes instruction-based tool restrictions from host permission controls and identifies what still needs runtime testing.

## Why this note is merged

A review of the current primary documentation on October 17, 2026 found that the useful instructions-versus-enforcement distinction overlaps with existing source audits. The old note did not establish a separate finding that would justify another indexable article.

The original claims that four products converged on one workflow, that adoption and benchmark figures settled product differences, that retry costs determined real economics, and that Grok Build had the described capabilities were not substantiated by the inspected primary sources. Those comparisons and examples are withdrawn; this page is not a current product, pricing, benchmark, or safety guide.

The original June 24, 2026 date and URL are preserved as a noncommercial historical notice. For the narrower execution-boundary finding, use the linked HarnessRouter audit.
