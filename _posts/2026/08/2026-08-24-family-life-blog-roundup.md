Most of what I publish here is about agents, harnesses, and the systems I build. But engineering isn't the whole of a life, and I keep a second, much more ordinary blog in Korean — everyday notes about raising a small kid, cafes worth the drive around Dongtan, a few restaurants, and the occasional diary entry.

I'm collecting the recent posts here for two reasons: so I can find them from one place, and so the two sides of what I write aren't completely walled off from each other. If you only came for the agent stuff, this is your cue to skip — but if you're curious what the non-terminal hours look like, the links below are the honest version.
